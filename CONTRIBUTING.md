# Contributing

By submitting a contribution to Foundation 14, you represent that you have the
right to submit it and agree that the contribution is licensed under the
license that applies to the material you add or modify.

Original Foundation 14 code, tools, scripts, prototypes, localization,
configuration, and other required game data are licensed under the Reciprocal
Public License 1.5 (`RPL-1.5`) unless a file, asset metadata file, or notice
states otherwise.

Contributions to upstream Space Station 14, RobustToolbox, third-party code, or
third-party assets must preserve the applicable upstream licenses, copyright
notices, and attribution metadata.

Do not submit material copied or derived from the SCP Wiki or other third-party
works unless the material is compatible with this project and includes the
required license and attribution information. SCP-derived material must not be
treated as Foundation 14-owned RPL material unless the contributor separately
owns the relevant rights and clearly licenses that original contribution under
RPL-1.5.

Keep SCP-derived material separately identifiable where practical. If a file or
asset metadata entry contains SCP-derived material, include the applicable
source license and attribution in that file, metadata entry, or an adjacent
notice.
